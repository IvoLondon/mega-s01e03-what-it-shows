What belongs in each ticket

Parser: the shared parser reads dates month first although the tenant writes day first. App and email reports, and booking-v2 form reports, take this path for the tenants in this export. For example, Harbour Dental’s app report R-41200 says 7 April became 4 July. With its en-GB tenant settings, 07/04/2026 must stay 7 April, not become 4 July.

Form hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY while the form reads the input in the tenant’s day-first order. This affects form reports from Alder Physio (de-DE), Copper Studio (es-ES), and Pale Harbour (pt-PT). R-41241 says an Alder client booked for 6 August arrived on 8 June: following the hint produces 08/06/2026, which the form reads as 8 June. The app and email channels for those same tenants still belong to the parser ticket.

Use the tenant’s locale and form version from src/lib/data/tenants, together with the report’s channel. meta.browserLocale describes the reporter’s browser, and meta.intakeTag is intake’s guess. Neither determines the cause.

Replace subject-only triage with evidence

The triage page still tells the desk to merge matching subjects and to merge when unsure. Its old review date triggers the layout’s stale warning. The accepted decision 0001 records the original choice and its tradeoff; keep that historical record unchanged.

For this incident, route by demonstrated cause instead:

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report ID before counting reports.
  2. Look up the tenant, date order, form version, and channel. Read the body and record the input date and observed result when available.
  3. Merge only when the evidence establishes the same cause. Subject wording can differ; matching subjects can still describe different causes.
  4. Keep unknown tenants and unresolved mechanisms separate for investigation. Do not treat two missing classifications as the same cause.
  5. Keep the one-day-move reports linked to their current filing assignment while checking their mechanism. A new finding may require reclassification and revised ticket counts.
  6. Before operational use, compare this snapshot with the current desk queue and tenant configuration. Refresh the export and rebuild the page when either changes.
